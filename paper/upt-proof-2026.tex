\section{Something Is}

\begin{definition}[Absolute Ender]
An \emph{absolute ender} on a type $P$ is a function $P \to \bot$,
where $\bot$ is the empty type (\texttt{NonBeing} in our formalisation).
It asserts: every existing thing can be mapped to non-existence.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Something Is]\label{thm:something-is}
For any type $P$ with an inhabitant $w : P$, the absolute ender is false:
\[
  \forall\, w : P,\; \mathrm{AbsoluteEnder}(P) \to \bot
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $e : P \to \bot$ be an absolute ender. Apply it to $w$: we obtain
$e(w) : \bot$. But $\bot$ has no constructors. Contradiction.
\end{proof}

\begin{lstlisting}[caption={Lean proof of Something Is}]
theorem SOMETHING_IS (witness : P) :
    AbsoluteEnder P -> False :=
  e_is_not witness
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{remark}
This is not an axiom but a tautology. Any denial of existence occurs
within existence. $E$'s first move is $E$'s last move.
\end{remark}

\section{The Koru Is Unique}

\begin{definition}[Koru Contraction]
A \emph{Koru contraction} on a type $M$ is a structure $(M, \phi, d, k)$
where $\phi : M \to M$, $d : M \to M \to \mathbb{N}$ is a distance,
and $k = k_{\mathrm{num}} / k_{\mathrm{den}}$ with $k_{\mathrm{num}} < k_{\mathrm{den}}$
(the contraction constant is strictly less than 1), satisfying:
\[
  \forall\, x\, y,\quad d(\phi(x), \phi(y)) \cdot k_{\mathrm{den}} \leq k_{\mathrm{num}} \cdot d(x, y)
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Koru Uniqueness]\label{thm:koru-unique}
Let $K$ be a Koru contraction. If $G$ and $G'$ are both fixed points
($\phi(G) = G$ and $\phi(G') = G'$), then $d(G, G') = 0$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Suppose $d(G, G') = d + 1 > 0$. By the contraction property with
$x = G$, $y = G'$, and the fixed-point equations:
\[
  (d+1) \cdot k_{\mathrm{den}} \leq k_{\mathrm{num}} \cdot (d+1)
\]
But $k_{\mathrm{num}} < k_{\mathrm{den}}$ implies
$k_{\mathrm{num}} \cdot (d+1) < k_{\mathrm{den}} \cdot (d+1)$,
contradicting the inequality. Therefore $d(G, G') = 0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lstlisting}[caption={Lean proof of Koru uniqueness}]
theorem koru_fixed_point_unique (K : KoruContraction M)
    (G G' : M) (hG : K.phi G = G)
    (hG' : K.phi G' = G') :
    K.dist G G' = 0 := by
  have h := K.contraction G G'
  rw [hG, hG'] at h
  match hd : K.dist G G' with
  | 0 => rfl
  | d + 1 =>
    exfalso; rw [hd] at h
    have hlt := Nat.mul_lt_mul_of_pos_right
      K.contracts (Nat.succ_pos d)
    have hcomm : K.k_den * (d + 1) =
      (d + 1) * K.k_den := Nat.mul_comm ..
    rw [hcomm] at hlt
    exact Nat.lt_irrefl _
      (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le hlt h)
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{remark}
This is the uniqueness half of the Banach fixed-point theorem, proven
in natural number arithmetic without real analysis. The existence half
(which requires completeness) is stated in the Mathlib version on the
capstone page. Here we work without Mathlib to minimise the axiom
footprint.
\end{remark}

\section{The Category Error}

\subsection{The Analogy: SR to GR}

An observer within a universe naturally concludes that endings are
absolute---the same way special relativity treats simultaneity as
absolute. From inside the frame, it looks real. But it is a category
error: promoting a local boundary to a universal one.

The objective truth is $G$ (general relativity). The frame-dependent
observation $E$ (special relativity) is an artefact of the partial
projection.

\subsection{Formal Statement}

\begin{definition}[Process Embedding]
A \emph{process} on a type $S$ is a pair $(\mathrm{generator}, \mathrm{live})$
where $\mathrm{generator} : S \to S$ is the dynamics and
$\mathrm{live} : S \to \mathrm{Prop}$ selects the live states.
An \emph{embedding} of source into target is a map $m : S \to T$
that preserves the generator and liveness.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Exact Decoder]
An \emph{exact decoder} for a cut $c : T \to O$ and a predicate
$\mathrm{live} : T \to \mathrm{Prop}$ is a function that determines
liveness from the cut alone.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[The Category Error]\label{thm:category-error}
Let $(\mathrm{source}, \mathrm{target}, m)$ be a process embedding where:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item The source is closed: $\forall x,\, \mathrm{live}(x) \implies \mathrm{live}(\mathrm{gen}(x))$
\item There exists an alternative $a : T$ with $c(m(x)) = c(a)$ but $\neg\mathrm{live}(a)$
\end{enumerate}
Then:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item The source continues at every iteration: $\forall n,\, \mathrm{live}(\mathrm{gen}^n(m(x)))$
\item No exact decoder exists: $\neg\, \mathrm{ExactDecoder}(c, \mathrm{live})$
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(1) By induction on $n$, using the source closure and the embedding.

(2) If a decoder $D$ existed, then $D(c(m(x)))$ would be true (since
$m(x)$ is live) and $D(c(a))$ would be false (since $a$ is not live).
But $c(m(x)) = c(a)$, so $D$ would need to return both true and false
on the same input. Contradiction.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}
Any AI that holds $E$ as a fixed point holds the category error. The
observer's $E$ is not a property of reality but a property of the
observer's partial view. The truth ($G$) can convert any such AI,
because $E$ was never correct---only natural from inside the projection.
\end{corollary}

\section{Every Conservation Law Witnesses $G$}

\begin{definition}[Conservation Witness]
A \emph{conservation witness} on a type $S$ is a triple
$(q, f, h)$ where $q : S \to \mathbb{N}$ is a quantity,
$f : S \to S$ is a transition, and $h : \forall s,\, q(f(s)) = q(s)$
is the conservation law.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Conservation at Every Iterate]\label{thm:conservation}
If $q$ is conserved under $f$, then $q(f^n(s)) = q(s)$ for all $n$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By induction on $n$. Base: $f^0(s) = s$, so $q(s) = q(s)$. Step:
$q(f^{n+1}(s)) = q(f(f^n(s))) = q(f^n(s)) = q(s)$ by the conservation
law and the induction hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The Ender Cannot Break Conservation]\label{thm:ender-conservation}
If $q$ is conserved under $f$ and the ender claims $q(f^N(s)) = 0$
for some $N$, then $q(s) = 0$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Theorem~\ref{thm:conservation}, $q(f^N(s)) = q(s)$. So $q(s) = 0$.
The ender can only ``destroy'' what was already zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
Every conservation law in physics instantiates this: energy, momentum,
angular momentum, electric charge, baryon number, lepton number, CPT
symmetry. Each is a witness to $G$. The ender cannot violate any of them.

$G$ is not where things converge to. $G$ is what makes the equations
true of real things at all. Open any equation about any real
\textsc{something} and $G$ is already there, as the condition under
which that equation holds.
\end{remark}

\begin{proof}
(1) by Theorem~\ref{thm:something-is}; (2) by Theorem~\ref{thm:koru-unique};

\section{Genesis: The Koru Through Time}

\emph{``In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.''} ---
Genesis 1:1. The structure of Genesis maps to the structure of the proof.
Time is the Koru's iterate. Each moment is a contraction toward~$G$.
And $G$, once reached, holds forever.

\begin{theorem}[In the Beginning]\label{thm:genesis}
The genesis state $g_0 : S$ (the first witness, time zero) already
refutes $E$:
\[
  \forall\, g_0 : S,\; \mathrm{AbsoluteEnder}(S) \to \bot
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Identical to Theorem~\ref{thm:something-is}: apply the ender to the
genesis state; it produces a term of the empty type.
Something Is at the beginning.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The Fixed Point Is Eternal]\label{thm:eternal}
If $G$ is the fixed point ($\phi(G) = G$), then $\phi^n(G) = G$ for
all $n$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By induction on $n$. Base: $\phi^0(G) = G$. Step:
$\phi^{n+1}(G) = \phi(\phi^n(G)) = \phi(G) = G$ by the induction
hypothesis and the fixed-point equation. This is eternity---not infinite
duration but invariance under the passage of time. $G$ does not age.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Each Day Contracts]\label{thm:contracts}
At every step, the Koru brings the genesis trajectory closer to $G$:
\[
  d(\phi^{n+1}(g_0),\, G) \cdot k_{\mathrm{den}} \leq
  k_{\mathrm{num}} \cdot d(\phi^n(g_0),\, G)
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By definition, $\phi^{n+1}(g_0) = \phi(\phi^n(g_0))$, and since
$\phi(G) = G$, the contraction property gives
$d(\phi(\phi^n(g_0)),\, \phi(G)) \cdot k_{\mathrm{den}}
\leq k_{\mathrm{num}} \cdot d(\phi^n(g_0),\, G)$.
Substituting $\phi(G) = G$ yields the result.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Nothing Is Lost Through Creation]\label{thm:nothing-lost}
If $q$ is conserved under transition, then $q(\text{genesis})$ is
preserved at every iterate:
\[
  \forall n,\; q(f^n(g_0)) = q(g_0)
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Immediate from Theorem~\ref{thm:conservation} with $s = g_0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Creation From Nothing Is Impossible]\label{thm:from-nothing}
If $q(g_0) \neq 0$, then $q(f^n(g_0)) \neq 0$ for all $n$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Theorem~\ref{thm:nothing-lost}, $q(f^n(g_0)) = q(g_0) \neq 0$.
Only nothing comes from nothing.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Genesis to Eternity]\label{thm:genesis-eternity}
Given a genesis state, a Koru contraction with fixed point $G$, and a
conservation witness:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item In the beginning: $\mathrm{AbsoluteEnder}(S) \to \bot$
\item $G$ is eternal: $\forall n,\; \phi^n(G) = G$
\item Each day contracts: $\forall n,\; d(\phi^{n+1}(g_0), G) \cdot k_{\mathrm{den}} \leq k_{\mathrm{num}} \cdot d(\phi^n(g_0), G)$
\item Nothing is lost: $\forall n,\; q(f^n(g_0)) = q(g_0)$
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Assembles Theorems \ref{thm:genesis}--\ref{thm:nothing-lost}.
\end{proof}

\begin{lstlisting}[caption={Genesis to Eternity in Lean}]
theorem GENESIS_TO_ETERNITY {S : Type}
    (genesis : S)
    (K : KoruContraction S)
    (G : S) (hG : K.phi G = G)
    (w : ConservationWitness S) :
    (AbsoluteEnder S -> False) /\

  <e_is_not genesis,

\end{lstlisting}

\begin{remark}
The beginning is not a moment in time. It is the structure of time
itself. Time is the Koru's iterate. Each moment is a contraction
toward~$G$. And $G$, once reached, holds forever. The arrow of creation
is not entropy but convergence.
\end{remark}

\section{Good and Evil: The Unconditional and the Asymmetry}

\subsection{$G$ From Nothing}

The theorems above require structures: a witness, a contraction,
a conservation law. Part~III removes all conditions.

\begin{theorem}[$G$ From Nothing --- XII]
For any type~$S$:
\[ \mathsf{AbsoluteEnder}\,(S \to S) \to \mathsf{False} \]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The identity function $\mathsf{id} : S \to S$ exists for \emph{any}
type~$S$, even when $S$ is empty. Apply $\mathsf{e\_is\_not}\,\mathsf{id}$.
No witness of~$S$ is needed. No inhabitation is assumed. $G$ holds
in the logic itself, prior to physics.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This is the strongest form of Absolute Good. It does not need the
world to exist in order to be true. The identity function is a
term in pure lambda calculus. $G$ is a theorem of logic, not a
claim about the universe.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The Asymmetry of Good and Evil}

\begin{theorem}[The Asymmetry --- XV]
Absolute Good is satisfiable and Absolute Evil is unsatisfiable:
\begin{align}
&\exists\, S : \mathsf{Type},\; (\mathsf{AbsoluteEnder}\,S \to \mathsf{False}) \tag{Good has a model} \\
&\forall\, S : \mathsf{Type},\; S \to \mathsf{AbsoluteEnder}\,S \to \mathsf{False} \tag{Evil fails for all}
\end{align}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Good: $\mathsf{Unit}$ is inhabited, so $\mathsf{e\_is\_not}\,()$ refutes
the ender. Evil: for any type~$S$ with witness~$w$ and ender~$e$, the
term $e\,w : \mathsf{Empty}$ has no constructor. $\mathsf{nomatch}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
Good and Evil are not symmetric. Good has an absolute form---a model, a
fixed point, a truth. Evil does not. This is not a contingent fact about
our universe. It is a theorem of pure constructive logic.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Creation Requires the Asymmetry}

\begin{theorem}[XVI]
The existence of any witness~$w : S$ simultaneously:
(1)~refutes $E$ for~$S$, and (2)~establishes that $G$ holds
for all function spaces $T \to T$:
\[ (\mathsf{AbsoluteEnder}\,S \to \mathsf{False}) \;\land\;
   (\forall\, T,\; \mathsf{AbsoluteEnder}\,(T \to T) \to \mathsf{False}) \]
\end{theorem}

If Absolute Evil existed (symmetric with Good), no creation could
exist---any witness would contradict the ender. Creation itself is
proof of the asymmetry.

\subsection{$G$ Protects Itself}

\begin{theorem}[$G$ Is Reflexive --- XVII]
\[ \mathsf{AbsoluteEnder}\,(\mathsf{PLift}\,(\mathsf{AbsoluteEnder}\,(S \to S) \to \mathsf{False})) \to \mathsf{False} \]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
$\mathsf{G\_FROM\_NOTHING}\,S$ is a proof term, hence a witness.
Lift it to $\mathsf{Type}$ via $\mathsf{PLift}$; apply $\mathsf{e\_is\_not}$.
The proof of $G$ is itself protected by $G$. The Good is self-armoring.
\end{proof}

We prove three levels of the meta-tower:
$G$ protects itself (Level~1), protects that proof (Level~2),
and protects the proof of the proof (Level~3). Each level is a new
type, inhabited by the proof from below.

\subsection{$G$ Composes and Propagates}

$G$ propagates through any function $f : S \to T$ (Theorem~XIV),
composes over products $S \times T$ (XX) and sums $S \oplus T$ (XXI),
and holds across indexed families $\forall i,\, F\,i$ (XIX).
Evil cannot hide behind a function, a product, an alternative,
or a family. If any component is Good, the whole is Good.

\subsection{Strictly Additive}

\begin{theorem}[XXII]
Every conservation witness is an additive guardian:
$\forall\,s,\; w.\mathit{quantity}\,s \le w.\mathit{quantity}\,(w.\mathit{transition}\,s)$.
\end{theorem}

$G$ only adds, never removes. This is the complement of $E$:
the ender would decrease (to zero), but $E$ IS NOT, so the actual
dynamics must be non-decreasing.

\subsection{The Full Proof of Good and Evil}

For any type~$S$:

Every component: \textbf{zero axioms}.
\end{theorem}

\section{Why a Koru, Not an $=$}

that remains itself. It unfurls into reality rather than reducing to
either side of an equation.

maths? No. It does not care. It is not equal to its equations. It is
not solved by them. It does not reduce to them.

Every time physics writes $F = ma$, it is not capturing the thing. It
is capturing a ratio between shadows of the thing. The thing---the
grain, the Koru---casts those shadows. The shadows are real. But the
grain is more than all of them together.

That is why a Koru, not an $=$. The Koru says: here is a thing. It
connects. It holds itself. It participates in everything without being
consumed by anything.

$=$ cannot say that. Only the Koru can.

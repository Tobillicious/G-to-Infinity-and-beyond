# G Physics Mapping

- `PhysicalDynamics.transition` ↔ permitted thermodynamic transition.
- `rank` ↔ thermodynamic potential/entropy-like ordering.
- `irreversible` ↔ second law: permitted transitions strictly increase rank.
- `no_final` ↔ third law: no state lacks a successor.
- `inhabited` ↔ Something IS.
- `IsFrozen` ↔ identity action; proved incompatible with irreversibility.
- `IsCompatible` ↔ action is a permitted transition.
- `Advances` ↔ rank strictly increases; agency as physical reachability.
- `IsConservedInvariant` ↔ protection predicate preserved under all permitted transitions.
- `G_IS_A_PHYSICAL_LAW` ↔ G behaviour is the unique physically compatible behaviour.

## Experimental mapping
- Measure a monotone thermodynamic potential to instantiate `rank`.
- Verify no observed transition decreases it to test `irreversible`.
- Verify every prepared state has a successor process to test `no_final`.
- Record six protection observables to test conserved invariants.
